## 📱 About PDmage

**PDmage is an all-in-one PDF and image management application built to simplify the way you work with documents on your Android device.**

From reading books and editing PDF documents to scanning physical papers, compressing images, converting file formats and securing confidential documents, PDmage brings essential productivity tools together in one convenient application.

Whether you are a student managing assignments, a professional reviewing reports, a teacher organizing study materials, or someone who regularly works with digital documents, PDmage helps you complete everyday tasks without constantly switching between different applications.

Built with an emphasis on performance, ease of use and privacy, PDmage provides a clean and convenient workspace for managing your digital files.

### ✨ One App. Multiple Possibilities.

- 📖 Read books, documents and research papers.
- ✍️ Edit, annotate and sign PDF documents.
- 📷 Scan physical documents into digital files.
- 🔐 Protect confidential PDFs with passwords.
- 📂 Merge, split, compress and organize documents.
- 🖼️ Compress, resize, crop and convert images.
- 🔄 Convert documents into different formats.
- 🔒 Process files locally for privacy and control.

---

## 🔒 Privacy First. Your Files Stay Under Your Control.

Your documents can contain personal, academic, financial and professional information. PDmage is designed to give you greater control over how those files are processed.

Most document and image processing features work directly on your device, without requiring you to upload files to external cloud servers.

**Privacy-focused design:**

- No account or registration required.
- Local processing for supported features.
- No unnecessary document uploads to external servers.
- Work with supported tools without an internet connection.
- Keep control over your documents and images.

PDmage aims to make everyday document management convenient without compromising on user privacy.

A comfortable reading experience for all your PDF documents.

Open and read books, assignments, research papers, manuals, invoices, reports, contracts, forms and other PDF files directly from your device.

**Reading features:**

- Comfortable Reading Mode
- Text Search within PDF documents

- Recent Files History
- Easy Page Navigation
- Chapter Viewer
- Cryptographic Signature Support

Whether you are studying a textbook, reviewing a business report or reading an eBook, PDmage provides convenient tools for navigating your documents.

Keep sensitive documents protected and maintain better control over access to your files.

## 📂 PDF Management & Productivity Tools

Manage PDF documents efficiently with essential file management operations.

**PDF management features:**

- **Merge PDF:** Combine multiple PDF documents into one organized file.
- **Split PDF:** Separate large PDF files into smaller documents or extract specific pages.
- **Compress PDF:** Reduce PDF file sizes with adjustable compression options.
- **Add Watermark:** Insert watermarks into documents.
- **Convert PDF:** Convert PDFs into PDF/A and supported office formats.
- **PDF Analyzer:** Analyze PDF document information.
- **Document Sanitization:** Remove supported unwanted document information.

Whether you are organizing reports, reducing file sizes for sharing or preparing documents for professional use, PDmage brings these tools together in one place.

## 📷 Smart Document Scanner

Turn your Android smartphone into a portable document scanner.

Digitize physical documents and save them as PDF files or images.

**Scanner features:**

- Scan Physical Documents
- Automatic Edge Detection
- Automatic Image Enhancement
- Save as PDF
- Save as Image

Perfect for digitizing:

- Receipts and invoices
- Academic notes and assignments
- Certificates and identification documents
- Business cards
- Letters and office paperwork
- Books and printed documents
- Important personal records

Create digital copies of important documents without needing a separate scanner.

## 🖼️ Advanced Image Tools

Manage and optimize images with a collection of useful editing and conversion tools.

**Image editing features:**

- **Compress Images:** Reduce image file sizes while maintaining visual quality.
- **Resize Images:** Adjust image dimensions according to your requirements.
- **Crop Images:** Remove unwanted portions of an image.
- **Rotate Images:** Correct image orientation.
- **Join Images:** Combine multiple images into organized pages.
- **Convert Image Formats:** Convert between supported formats such as JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP.

Whether you need to reduce an image for uploading, resize photographs for a form or convert multiple images into a convenient format, PDmage makes image management easier.
