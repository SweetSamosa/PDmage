## 🔒 100% Offline & Privacy-Focused

Your data stays yours. PDmage processes everything locally on your device, meaning:

- No file uploads to external servers
- No data collection or tracking
- Full control over your documents and images

Perfect for users who value security and confidentiality.

---

## 📄 Powerful PDF Tools

Manage your PDF files with ease using a complete suite of features:

- **Compress PDF:** Reduce file size with adjustable quality settings while maintaining readability.
- **Merge PDFs:** Combine multiple documents into one organized file.
- **Split PDFs:** Extract specific pages or break large PDFs into smaller ones.
- **Convert to PDF:** Turn images and documents into high-quality PDFs instantly.

---

## 🖼️ Advanced Image Tools

Edit and optimize your images directly on your device:

- **Compress Images:** Reduce size without significant quality loss.
- **Resize Images:** Adjust dimensions to meet your needs.
- **Crop Images:** Remove unwanted areas with precision.
- **Convert Images:** Convert images into different formats.
